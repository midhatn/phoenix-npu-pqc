# License history

Repository revisions published before the project-wide Apache License 2.0
transition were offered under the MIT License recorded in those revisions.
Permissions already granted for those revisions are not withdrawn.

Beginning with the transition revision, original Phoenix NPU PQC work that
does not carry a different file-level notice is offered under the Apache
License 2.0. File-level SPDX identifiers and third-party notices remain
authoritative when they differ from the project default.

The former project MIT text is retained in `LICENSES/MIT.txt` for historical
reference and for files that remain explicitly MIT.
